Neural Network Topology

The architecture implements a multi-layer perceptron with dropout regularization to prevent overfitting on tabular cytometry distributions:

  • Input Layer: 30 standardized morphometric inputs
  • Hidden Layer 1: Dense (32 units, ReLU activation)
  • Regularization: Dropout (25% rate)
  • Hidden Layer 2: Dense (16 units, ReLU activation)
  • Output Layer: Dense (1 unit, Sigmoid probability)

Explainable AI (SHAP Framework)

To ensure model transparency and clinical auditability, every inference query is routed through a background-sampled Shapley Additive Explanations (SHAP) explainer.

This provides quantitative feature importance coefficients for each individual patient specimen, displaying exactly how much each nuclear metric nudged the assessment toward benignity or malignancy.

Clinical Research Decision-Support Notice

This digital pathology decision-support tool is designed for adjunctive analytical assistance. Results do not constitute a standalone histological diagnosis. Final diagnosis must incorporate full clinical triple-assessment, mammographic imaging, and formal histopathological examination by a board-certified pathologist.

Model Overview & Specifications

Built for fast and reliable computer-aided screening of digitized fine needle aspirate (FNA) cytology specimens.

Dense MLP Backbone

Uses fully-connected layers with dropout regularization and ReLU activations, providing high feature extraction quality with minimal latency.

Two-Phase Training

Initial training optimized feature weighting across 100 epochs, followed by calibrated loss weighting to prioritize high sensitivity on malignant cases.

97.37% Holdout Accuracy

Evaluated on an independent validation split of 114 test specimens with balanced sensitivity and specificity across malignant and benign biopsies.

Standardized Preprocessing

All 30 cytometric metrics are standardized using z-score normalization fitted on reference training distributions to ensure zero distribution drift.

Dataset & Optimization Specifications
Dataset Reference Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer (WDBC) Database (569 clinical biopsy specimens)
Input Features 30 standardized nuclear morphometric measurements (10 Mean, 10 SE, 10 Worst)
Model Architecture Deep Feed-Forward Multi-Layer Perceptron (Dense 32 → Dropout 0.25 → Dense 16 → Dense 1)
Explainability Protocol Shapley Additive Explanations (SHAP) with background distribution sampling (k=100)
Inference Latency ~12 ms per specimen on standard CPU runtime
